Apex Solutions: Internal Operations & Employee Policy
1. Remote Work and Hybrid Schedule
Apex Solutions operates on a "Hybrid-First" model. All employees are expected to be in the main office on Tuesdays and Thursdays for collaborative syncs. Employees may work from home on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays.

Core Hours: Regardless of location, all employees must be online and available between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST.

Stipend: A one-time $500 home-office equipment stipend is available to all full-time staff after 90 days of employment.

2. Vacation and Paid Time Off (PTO)
Accrual: Full-time employees accrue 15 days of PTO per calendar year, earned at a rate of 1.25 days per month.

Rollover: A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over to the following calendar year.

Notice: For any leave longer than 3 consecutive days, employees must submit a request via the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.

3. Professional Development
We believe in continuous learning. The company will reimburse up to $2,000 per year for approved certifications, bootcamps, or conferences.

Approval: All courses must be submitted to your direct manager for approval before enrollment to qualify for reimbursement.

4. Communication Etiquette
Slack: Primary internal communication happens on Slack. Use public channels for project-related updates to ensure transparency.

Email: Use email for formal external communication with clients and vendors only.

Response Time: Internal Slack messages should ideally be acknowledged within 4 business hours.

5. Appointment Booking Protocols
When booking meetings for clients:

Standard consultations are 30 minutes.

Technical deep-dives are 60 minutes.

No appointments should be booked before 9:00 AM or after 5:00 PM local time.
